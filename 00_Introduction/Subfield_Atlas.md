# Subfield Atlas

Robotics is not one subject. It is a meeting point for mathematics, mechanics, electronics, computing, biology, design and ethics, and it keeps growing into places nobody planned for. This atlas maps the part of it this textbook covers so far, grouped from the core stack every robot needs, to the bodies robots take, the places they work, the people they work with, and the frontier.

Every note names its subfields in its `subfield:` frontmatter, so this page rebuilds itself as the textbook grows. Subfields with no notes yet are listed on purpose.
